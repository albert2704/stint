MENU BAR

Always there. Never in the way.

The countdown stays in your menu bar while you work in other apps. Click it to pause, skip, change rhythm, or turn the sound down. Close the window and Stint keeps counting.

SOUND

Music that knows when to stop.

Three original instrumental loops come built in. Add your own audio files or a YouTube video or live stream. Turn on Play with timer and music follows your focus sessions and breaks, pausing when the timer pauses or finishes.

Music never starts on its own when you open Stint.

Questions

How much does Stint cost?+

Stint is free and open source under the MIT license. No subscription, trial period, or account. You can use it, inspect the code, and make it your own.

Which Macs does it run on?+

Stint targets macOS 12 or later. One universal download includes Apple Silicon and Intel support. This preview has been checked on Apple Silicon; physical Intel and macOS 12 testing are still pending. See verification details.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. The timer, built-in loops, your own audio files and your progress all work without a connection. Only YouTube playback needs the internet.

What happens when I close the window?+

Stint keeps running in the menu bar. Click the countdown for the controls, or use the default Control + Option + Command + S shortcut to open the timer panel. You can change shortcuts in Settings. Choose Quit Stint to stop it.

Will music start by itself?+

Never when you open the app. With Play with timer on, music starts with a focus session or break and pauses when the timer pauses or finishes. With it off, playback is entirely up to you. YouTube also pauses when you hide its player.

What counts toward my progress?+

Only focus stints you finish. Skipping, resetting or jumping ahead never adds to your count.
