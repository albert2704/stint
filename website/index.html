Questions

How much does Stint cost?+

Stint is free and open source under the MIT license. No subscription, trial period, or account. You can use it, inspect the code, and make it your own.

Which Macs does it run on?+

Stint targets macOS 12 or later. One universal download includes Apple Silicon and Intel support. This beta has been checked on Apple Silicon; physical Intel and macOS 12 testing are still pending. See verification details.

Does it work offline?+

Yes. The timer, built-in loops, your own audio files and your progress all work without a connection. YouTube playback and browsing need an internet connection.

What happens when I close the window?+

Stint keeps running in the menu bar. Click the countdown for the controls, or use the default Control + Option + Command + S shortcut to open the timer panel. You can change shortcuts in Settings. Choose Quit Stint to stop it.

Will music start by itself?+

Never when you open the app. With Play with timer on, music starts with a focus session or break and pauses when the timer pauses or finishes. With it off, playback is entirely up to you. YouTube also pauses when you hide its player.

How do I set up website blocking?+

Open Settings → Block distractions, choose your apps and domains, and save the list. Choose Set up website blocker to reveal the installer in Finder, then open it and approve setup in Terminal with your administrator password. Stint never receives your password. No extension, certificate or paid Apple account is needed. Follow the setup guide.

Which browsers does the blocker support?+

Live Edge checks were completed for 0.2.0. Real browser enforcement has not been re-tested for 0.3.0. Chrome is designed to use the same macOS proxy settings, but remains unverified. Browsers must use those settings; VPNs and custom proxies are unsupported. Loaded pages and buffered video can remain visible. The settings affect all accounts using those network services, including Stint’s YouTube player. Choose a built-in or local track when blocking YouTube. HTTPS stays encrypted and Stint does not log your browsing.

Can I stop blocking before time is up?+

Yes. Pause the timer, take a break, or confirm Unblock this stint. The next focus session uses your saved list again. The helper also restores network settings when Stint quits or crashes. If the helper is disabled and access does not return, the recovery guide explains how to restore access and remove it.

What counts toward my progress?+

Completed Pomodoro focus intervals and Flow sessions finished with Take break. Skipping, resetting or jumping ahead never adds to your count. Weekly totals use recorded focus time and the mean length of completed sessions.
