# Stint 0.2.0 beta 1

_Released 11 October 2026_

You can now block distracting apps and websites while your focus timer runs. The website blocker keeps your browser open, so you can still use allowed tabs.

## Highlights

* **App blocking:** choose apps to hide when you switch to them, preserving their unsaved work.
* **Website blocking:** add domains and their subdomains to your blocklist. A free local helper refuses blocked connections without a browser extension or Apple Developer subscription.
* **Control during a stint:** pausing or taking a break releases blocking. You can also confirm **Unblock this stint** to allow distractions until the next focus interval.

## Improvements

* Website setup and removal reveal a command in Finder for you to open and approve in Terminal.
* The helper saves your previous proxy settings for recovery. HTTPS stays encrypted, and Stint does not log your browsing activity.
* Helper readiness and active website protection have separate status messages.

## Fixes

* Replaced the earlier source preview's hosts approach, which allowed YouTube to remain accessible in an existing Edge session. Live Edge checks now show blocked YouTube navigation while an allowed GitHub tab continues to work.
* Verified network setting restoration on pause, app crash, normal quit and helper restart.

## Upgrade notes

You can get the [universal DMG](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.2.0-beta.1/Stint-0.2.0-macos-universal.dmg), [ZIP](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.2.0-beta.1/Stint-0.2.0-macos-universal.zip), and [checksums](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.2.0-beta.1/SHA256SUMS.txt) from GitHub. The app targets macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. It is ad hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple; see the [installation guide](../install.md) for first launch approval.

Quit the previous app before replacing it. Version 0.1.1 data uses the same app identity and locations; a local replacement with this release app preserved the timer, progress and music. Upgrading from 0.1.0 still starts fresh, as described in the [0.1.1 notes](v0.1.1.md).

For websites, you can open **Settings → Block distractions → Set up website blocker** and complete the highlighted command. Administrator approval is required. If you used the older source preview helper, this step upgrades it. App blocking does not require that helper.

This is a beta. Edge was tested; Chrome, physical Intel Macs, macOS 12 and the downloaded app's Gatekeeper flow remain unverified. Browsers must use macOS proxy settings. VPNs and existing custom proxy setups are unsupported, and already loaded pages or buffered media may remain visible. Settings affect all accounts using those network services, including Stint's YouTube music. You can choose a built in or local track when blocking YouTube.

See [blocking and emergency recovery](../blocking.md) and the [verification record](../testing.md#version-020-beta-verification) before enabling website protection. If you uninstall Stint later, **Remove helper** restores its network settings and removes the background service; deleting the app alone does not remove that helper.
