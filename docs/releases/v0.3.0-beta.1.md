# Stint 0.3.0 Beta 1

_Released 20 October 2026_

You can now save the setup you use for different kinds of work, focus without a fixed countdown, and leave yourself a next step for your return.

## Highlights

- **Focus profiles:** Coding, Writing and Study prepare your timer, music, ambient mix and blocklist together. You can save, update, rename and delete your own profiles.
- **Flow mode:** Count focused time upward until you choose Take break. Stint records the session and suggests a break of one minute per five focused minutes, rounded up and bounded to 1 through 30 minutes.
- **Ambient mixer:** Blend Rain, Café hum and Brown noise at independent volumes, alongside music or on their own.
- **Weekly progress:** See focus time, finished sessions and average session length for each week, with an adjustable weekly target.
- **Pick up here:** Keep one optional sentence near the timer so you know where to resume.

## Improvements

- The menu bar panel opens your profiles, continuation editor and mixer.
- Applying a profile prepares an idle timer and silent audio settings. Unfinished time requires confirmation, and missing imported music has an explained fallback.
- Flow keeps the four stint cycle and pauses when the Mac sends a sleep event. App and website blocking use expiring protection while Flow runs.
- Your new settings and profiles stay on your Mac. The ambient loops are bundled original audio and need no streaming service.

## Fixes

- Fixed a problem where music could begin at its saved volume before its fade had started.
- Fixed a native timer update crash when a previous snapshot did not contain a completion count.

## Upgrade notes

You can download the [universal DMG](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.3.0-beta.1/Stint-0.3.0-macos-universal.dmg), [ZIP](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.3.0-beta.1/Stint-0.3.0-macos-universal.zip), and [checksums](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/releases/download/v0.3.0-beta.1/SHA256SUMS.txt). Quit the running app before replacing it in Applications.

Version 0.1.1 and later use the same app identity and data locations. Existing saves load with defaults for the new settings. Upgrading from 0.1.0 still starts fresh, as described in the [0.1.1 notes](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.1.1.md).

Flow restores paused from its last saved checkpoint after relaunch. Checkpoints save every 15 active seconds, so quitting or crashing can lose time since the last checkpoint. Pausing before quitting saves exact elapsed time.

This beta targets macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. It is ad hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/blob/main/docs/install.md) for first launch instructions and the [verification record](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/blob/main/docs/testing.md) for completed checks and limits.

Website blocking still needs the existing optional administrator helper. Profiles never install it automatically. Real website enforcement was last checked on 0.2.0 in Edge; it was not exercised again for this release. Chrome, physical Intel hardware, physical sleep and wake, and macOS 12 remain unverified. See [blocking and recovery](https://github.com/albert2704/stint/blob/main/docs/blocking.md).
