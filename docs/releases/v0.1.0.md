# Stint v0.1.0

_Released 10 October 2026 as a prerelease._

Stint brings your Pomodoro countdown to the Mac menu bar, with adjustable rhythms, focus music, and progress you can review at the end of the day. You can install this first downloadable package without Flutter or developer tools.

## Highlights

- **A timer within reach:** Start, pause, reset, or skip from the main window or the compact menu bar panel.
- **Your own rhythm:** Choose Quick focus, Classic, or Deep work, save custom durations, and adjust the current interval by a minute.
- **Music that follows the timer:** Use three original instrumental loops, your own audio files, or a YouTube link. Play with timer works during focus and breaks.
- **Completion alerts:** Hear your selected alert twice and use the Stint card to start the next interval when you are ready.
- **Progress at a glance:** Review completed stints, weekly activity, streaks, goal days, and total focus time. Set a daily goal from 1 to 16.
- **Custom shortcuts:** Record Start/Pause, Reset, Skip, and Show timer panel bindings that work across your Mac or only in Stint.

## Improvements

- Light, Dark, and System appearance, with layouts that fit a compact window.
- YouTube plays inside the menu bar panel and pauses when the panel hides.
- Reset, Skip, rhythm changes, and cycle jumps ask before discarding unfinished time.
- Timer state, settings, music choices, and completed stints are saved locally. No Stint account is needed.

This version is a **prerelease with an ad hoc signature**, without an Apple Developer ID signature or Apple notarization. Expect a Gatekeeper warning. If you trust the download, follow the [installation guide](../install.md) and [Apple's opening guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

You can quit any running copy, then copy Stint into Applications. The existing bundle identity and local data location are retained so earlier settings and history can continue to load. There is no automatic updater.

Shortcut delivery across other apps still needs physical keyboard verification across devices. System notification delivery after Quit depends on macOS permissions and Focus settings. YouTube requires internet access and a video that permits embedding. Android is not included.
